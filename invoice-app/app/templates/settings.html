Set these on the server (never in the browser). Any SMTP provider works: Amazon SES, Postmark, SendGrid, Mailgun, Resend, or Gmail with an app password.

SMTP_HOST=smtp.yourprovider.com
SMTP_PORT=587
SMTP_SECURITY=starttls    # starttls | ssl | none
SMTP_USERNAME=...
SMTP_PASSWORD=...
SMTP_FROM="Your Business <billing@yourdomain.com>"
